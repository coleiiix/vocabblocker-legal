The Gate

The Gate is a focus tool: when you open an app you’ve chosen to guard, it asks you to do one quick learning task before you can scroll, and unlocks that app for 15 minutes after a 3-second hold. On a Learn Day you learn a new word, spell it and write its meaning; on a Test Day you play one quick game with words you’ve saved. The app picks the mode each day from your progress. On the free plan, the Gate is a simple lock instead: a short pause, then a 3-second hold to open the app, with no word or game. It uses Apple’s Screen Time features and depends on your device’s settings and iOS.

  • You choose which apps are guarded, and you can turn the Gate off at any time.
  • Don’t guard apps you may need urgently, such as phone, messaging or emergency apps. The Gate isn’t designed for, and must not be relied on for, emergencies or safety.
  • We can’t promise the Gate will work at every moment or on every device, and we aren’t responsible for messages, notifications or content you miss while an app is guarded.
