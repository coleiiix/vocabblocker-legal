Privacy Policy

VocabBlocker helps you learn new words and scroll less. This policy explains what information VocabBlocker uses, where it is kept, who it is shared with, and how you can delete it. The short version: your learning data stays on your device, we don't run ads, we don't track you across apps, and we never sell your information.

Who we are

VocabBlocker is made by Harlan Roberts ("we", "us"). If you have any question about this policy or your information, contact us using the details at the end of this page.

Information you give us

During setup and while you use the app, you may give us:

  • Your first name, so the app can greet you. You can skip it.
  • Your answers in onboarding: your goals, how often you run into words you don’t know, roughly how many hours a day you scroll, which apps pull you in, and the topics you like.
  • Your level check and level test answers, used to set your vocabulary level.
  • The words you save, and your answers in games, quizzes and the Gate.

All of this is stored only on your device, in the app’s local storage. It is not sent to us.

Information the app creates as you use it

  • Your learning progress: words you’ve seen, saved words, mastery points and wreaths, streaks, levels and ranks, and game results.
  • Gate activity: when the Gate checked you, which of your guarded apps it was (as the app you chose in VocabBlocker, such as “Instagram”), whether it was a Learn Day or a Test Day, the word you learned or the game you played, and whether you stayed focused or opened the app.
  • Your Gate mode for the day. Each day the app decides on your device whether the Gate asks you to learn a new word (Learn Day) or play a game with your saved words (Test Day), based on how close your saved words are to mastered. This is worked out and stored only on your device.
  • Your settings, such as your theme and notification choices.

This information is also stored only on your device.

Sign in with Apple

Signing in is optional; the app works without it. If you choose Sign in with Apple, Apple gives the app a private user identifier and, if you allow it, your name and email address. You can use Apple’s “Hide My Email” so we only receive a private relay address.

Today this sign-in information is kept only on your device. We do not run servers that store your account. If we add cloud saving in the future, we will update this policy and ask for your permission before any of your information leaves your device.

Screen Time and the Gate

The Gate uses Apple’s Screen Time framework (Family Controls). You choose the apps to guard in Apple’s own app picker. Apple gives VocabBlocker only private tokens for those apps: we cannot see the names of the apps you picked, your usage, or anything you do inside them.

  • Screen Time information never leaves your device and is never sent to us or anyone else.
  • It is used only to show the Gate when you open a guarded app and to unlock that app for 15 minutes after you finish.
  • It is never used for advertising, marketing, profiling or analytics, and never sold.

You can turn the Gate off for any app in the Gate tab, or stop VocabBlocker’s Screen Time access at any time in iOS Settings → Screen Time.

Subscriptions and payments

Purchases (the yearly plan, the monthly plan and the free trial) are handled by Apple through your Apple ID. We never see or store your payment card or billing details.

To check whether you have an active subscription or free trial, restore purchases on a new device, and open Apple’s manage-subscription sheet, VocabBlocker uses RevenueCat (RevenueCat, Inc.), a subscription service provider. When you open the app or buy, RevenueCat receives:

  • A random anonymous ID that RevenueCat creates for your install (not your name or email).
  • Your purchase and subscription details from Apple: which plan, when it started, renewed, was cancelled or expires, the trial status and Apple’s transaction receipt.
  • Basic device and app information, such as your device model, iOS version, app version, country and language settings, and your IP address (used to work out your approximate country).

RevenueCat uses this only to tell VocabBlocker your subscription status and to run the service for us. It does not use it to advertise to you or to track you across other companies’ apps or websites, and it does not sell it. We do not link this ID to your Apple sign-in, your name or your learning data. RevenueCat is based in the United States, so this information may be processed there. RevenueCat’s own privacy policy (revenuecat.com/privacy) applies to the information it receives. The result (Premium or free) is kept on your device and decides which features you get.

Widgets

If you add the Word of the Day widget to your Home Screen or Lock Screen, VocabBlocker shares that day’s word, its definition and its example with the widget on your device so it can show them. This stays on your device and is not sent to us or anyone else. You can remove the widget at any time.

Notifications

If you allow notifications, reminders (for example, that your streak is at risk, that a word is close to Mastery, that there’s a game you haven’t tried, or that your trial ends tomorrow) are scheduled locally on your device. They are not sent from a server and use no information about you beyond what is already on your device. You can turn them off in the app’s Notifications settings or in iOS Settings → Notifications.

Word lookups

Most word definitions, examples and origins are built into the app. For some words, the app looks up extra details from Wiktionary (run by the Wikimedia Foundation) or the Free Dictionary API (dictionaryapi.dev). These requests contain only the word being looked up. Like any internet request, they also reveal your device’s IP address and basic network information to that service. Nothing about your account or progress is sent. Those services’ own privacy policies apply to the requests they receive.

Pronunciations are played from voice recordings built into VocabBlocker (the voices are AI-generated), so they work offline and need no internet request. Your choice of voice is stored only on your device. If a recording isn’t available for a word, your device’s built-in text-to-speech reads it instead, and that runs on your device too.

What we don’t do

  • We don’t show ads.
  • We don’t track you across other companies’ apps or websites, and we don’t use advertising identifiers.
  • We don’t use third-party analytics or advertising services. (RevenueCat, described above, only handles subscriptions.)
  • We don’t sell or rent your information, or share it for advertising.

How your information is used

Only to run the app for you: to pick and order your words at your level, track your mastery and streaks, run games, quizzes and the Gate, remember your settings, show your stats, send the reminders you turned on, and manage your subscription status. We do not use your information for any other purpose without asking you first.

Sharing

Because your learning information stays on your device, we don’t receive it and don’t share it. The only outside services the app talks to are Apple (sign-in, purchases, Screen Time and notifications, under Apple’s privacy policy), RevenueCat (your subscription status, as described above) and the word lookup services described above.

If we ever use a service provider that receives user data, such as cloud storage or an analytics tool, we will tell you here first, and we will only use providers that protect your data at least as well as this policy and Apple’s App Store rules require. We may disclose information if the law requires it, or as part of a merger or sale of the app, in which case this policy will continue to apply to it.

Keeping and deleting your information

Your information stays on your device until you delete it. You can:

  • Delete everything: in VocabBlocker, go to Profile → Account & data → Delete everything. This erases your progress, saved words, answers, settings and sign-in from your device and cancels its scheduled notifications. Deleting the VocabBlocker app does the same. (Neither cancels a subscription; see the Terms of Use.)
  • Stop using Sign in with Apple with VocabBlocker: iOS Settings → [your name] → Sign-In & Security → Sign in with Apple → VocabBlocker → Stop Using Apple ID.
  • Stop Screen Time access: turn the Gate off in the Gate tab, or use iOS Settings → Screen Time.
  • Turn off notifications in the app or in iOS Settings → Notifications.
  • Subscription records: RevenueCat keeps your anonymous ID and purchase history for as long as needed to run your subscription and meet tax and legal duties. Deleting the app does not erase them. To have them deleted, contact us and we will ask RevenueCat to delete them (your Apple purchase history with Apple is separate and kept by Apple).

Because we don’t keep your learning information on our own servers, there is no copy for us to delete. You can still contact us with any request or question about your data, and we will reply within 30 days.

Your choices and consent

The app asks before using Screen Time, notifications or Sign in with Apple, and you can say no and keep using the rest of the app. You can withdraw any of these permissions at any time in iOS Settings or as described above.

Your privacy rights

Depending on where you live (for example, under the GDPR in the EU and UK, or state laws such as the California Consumer Privacy Act), you may have the right to know what personal information is processed about you, to access, correct, delete or move it, to object to or restrict its use, and to complain to your local data protection authority. Since your learning information is kept on your device, you can see and delete it yourself, and you can contact us to exercise any of these rights, including for the subscription records RevenueCat holds for us. We do not sell or share personal information as those laws define it, and we will never treat you differently for using your rights.

Children

VocabBlocker is not directed to children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from them. Users from 13 to 17 should use the app with a parent’s or guardian’s permission. Learning data stays on the device and is not sent to us. If you are a parent or guardian and believe your child has given us personal information, contact us and we will help you remove it.

Security

Your information is protected by your device’s own security, including its passcode and encryption. Keep your device secure and up to date. No method of storage is perfectly secure, but because we don’t keep your learning data on our servers, it can’t be exposed in a breach of ours. Subscription information held by RevenueCat is protected by RevenueCat’s own security measures.

Changes to this policy

If we change how the app uses information, we will update this page and its date, and for important changes we will let you know in the app before they take effect. We will ask for your permission before using information in a new way.
